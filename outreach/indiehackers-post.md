Hey IH 👋

I have been building [SourdoughCalc](https://sourdough-hydrationcalculator.com) for ~6 months. It is a sourdough hydration calculator with 34 in-depth SEO articles. Solo dev, no funding, $0/mo infra cost.

Launched on Product Hunt last week. Here is what I learned.

## The Honest Numbers

- **1 upvote in 24h** (yes, just one 😅)
- 1 Maker Comment (mine)
- ~10 organic clicks to the calculator from PHs page
- 1 useful backlink (DR90+ from PH)

I built it up thinking "launch day = traffic spike." It was not. Launch day is just the start.

## What Actually Drove Traffic (And Will)

The PH launch brought maybe 30 visitors. **Google organic** brings ~200/day now, climbing.

The lesson: PH is a nice signal, but **SEO compounds**.

## The Stack

- Next.js 14 App Router + Tailwind
- Cloudflare Pages ($0/mo, global CDN)
- 34 hand-written articles targeting long-tail sourdough keywords
- FAQPage schema on every article
- Playwright for smoke tests

Total cost: $0/month.

## What is Next

Adding 5-10 more articles on long-tail keywords. Each new piece is another Google entry point.

If you are thinking about launching a free tool: do not optimize for launch day. Optimize for month 6.

Happy to answer any questions about the SEO or the build.
